Frequently asked questions

What happens when I hit my call limit?+

You'll be charged per additional call at your plan's rate ($1.25, $0.75, or $0.50 per call). Or upgrade to the next plan anytime — takes effect immediately. We notify you at 80% of your limit so there are no surprises.

Is there really no setup fee?+

None. Zero. Sign up, answer a few questions about your business, and your AI receptionist is live in under 10 minutes.

What happens after the free trial?+

After 7 days, you choose a plan and enter payment. If you do nothing, the trial simply ends — no charge, no commitment. Your call data and settings are saved for 30 days in case you come back.

Can I change plans later?+

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade anytime from your dashboard. Changes take effect immediately. If you upgrade mid-cycle, we prorate the difference.

Do spam calls count toward my limit?+

No. Our AI filters spam automatically. Only real conversations with real people count toward your call limit.
